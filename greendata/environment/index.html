Open data sources. Biodiversity: iNaturalist (research-grade observations) and GBIF. Soil: SoilGrids 2.0 (ISRIC, CC BY 4.0). Air quality and weather: Open-Meteo with Copernicus CAMS (CC BY 4.0). River discharge: GloFAS (Copernicus) via Open-Meteo. Basemap: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap.

Disclaimer. These are global models and citizen-science records, not official measurements. They do not replace data from SENAMHI, ANA, SERFOR, MINAM, OEFA or other authorities. Personal, educational project.
