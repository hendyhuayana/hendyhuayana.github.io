About

I work with geospatial data, remote sensing and climate risk. At OEFA, Peru's environmental enforcement agency, I build risk assessments, early warning workflows and dashboards using satellite and climate data. Most of my work revolves around El Niño risk, hazard susceptibility, precipitation forecasts and environmental monitoring.

I'm finishing my undergraduate thesis on heavy-metal removal from acid mine drainage, and I taught Geospatial Databases as a volunteer lecturer at Universidad Nacional Federico Villarreal (UNFV).
